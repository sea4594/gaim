Eight-round strategy experiment · beacon-v1

How to play
Gaim: Restoration

This rulebook describes the Phase 1 playable solo redesign. It is not the historical Storm Engine reducer, the old multiplayer Charge Loom v0.1 ruleset, or a completed co-op board game.

Start playing →

Objective and setup

Survive round 8 with Hull above zero, charge all three Beacon cells, and have at least one upgraded system online entering the last round. You start with Hull 6, Power 2, Coolant 2, Beacon 0/3 and all systems at level 0. All three storms are fixed, open-information puzzles intended for testing strategic decisions, not procedural replayability. Only one player makes the decisions in this phase.

Board and charges

The board has three lanes: Left, Center and Right. Each has FAR (depth 2) and NEAR (depth 1). Incoming charges arrive at FAR. Each charge has positive (+) or negative (−) polarity and strength 1–3. Non-held charges advance one position during resolution. Charges advancing from NEAR breach, disappear, and cancel opposite-polarity charge strength only within the same lane and round. Each matched unit generates 1 Power, capped at 8 stored Power. Unmatched strength is raw leakage and can damage Hull. Your next wave is visible in advance.

Round sequence

  1. Activate prior construction. Last round's system upgrade becomes active now, even if that system is offline.
  2. Reveal incoming charges. The current wave enters FAR.
  3. Choose up to two paid edits. No charge may receive more than one paid edit per round.
  4. Choose at most one 2-Power project. Either charge a Beacon cell immediately, or order one system level for next round. You cannot construct during round 8 because it would not activate in time. No construction if that system is currently offline. Projects spend Power before collisions, not newly generated Power.
  5. Choose your shielding. Spend 0, 1 or 2 Power to prevent damage this round. Shield Power is paid before resolving, even if you end up taking no damage. Your project and shield must both be affordable from stored Power at the start of the round.
  6. Optionally use Switchyard. An online Switchyard can shift one additional eligible charge for free; this executes after paid edits.
  7. Resolve movement and collisions. All non-held charges advance. Sum matched strengths and leakage by lane. Apply Power generation, shielding, Hull damage and new outages.
  8. Check loss or win. If Hull is zero or lower, you immediately lose. On round 8, all remaining charges also damage Hull by their combined strength. Survive and meet the objective to win; otherwise lose.

Paid charge interventions

  • SHIFT: Move a selected charge one lane to its left or right. Cannot leave the board.
  • HOLD: Stop one charge's movement for this round, increase its strength by one (maximum 3). Each charge can only be held once in its lifetime; holding creates future risk.
  • DAMP: Spend 1 Coolant to reduce one charge's strength by one, removing it if its strength becomes zero. Coolant starts at 2 and does not regenerate in this prototype.

Paid edits occur in the order selected. Two edits may target two different charges. You can always choose zero edits. The round preview applies all of your current orders and displays their consequences using only already visible storm information.

Infrastructure: distinct strategic investments

  • Dynamo: At level 1, if Left has any matching collision, gain +1 bonus Power. At level 2, if any lane matches, gain +2 bonus Power. Bonus is at most once per round. Dynamo must be online that round.
  • Aegis: Shields always absorb 1 damage per Power spent. With Aegis online, each shield Power instead absorbs 2 at level 1 or 3 at level 2. It gives no automatic protection: buying Aegis is useful only if you reserve Power for protection.
  • Switchyard: At level 1, SHIFT one FAR charge for free each round; at level 2, SHIFT one charge at FAR or NEAR for free. It must be online and the move must be legal.

Each system costs 2 Power per new level (maximum level 2), consumes the round's single project slot, and activates at the start of the following round.

System outages

If a lane leaks at least 2 total unmatched strength this round, its associated system goes offline for the following round: Left = Dynamo, Center = Aegis, Right = Switchyard. An offline system cannot provide its effect or receive construction during that round, but its levels are preserved. The system can recover on the next round if no fresh outage occurs. The final objective counts a system only if it was online entering round 8; an outage created by round 8's leakage does not retroactively disable it for that objective.

Worked example

If +3 and −2 charges breach together in Left, 2 units match and produce 2 Power; 1 positive strength leaks. Without shielding, Hull loses 1. If you spent 1 Shield Power earlier, that loss is prevented, whether or not Aegis is online. If the lane had leaked 2 or more, Dynamo would go offline next round.

If Power is 4 at the start of a round, you may charge a Beacon cell for 2 Power and spend 2 Power on shielding, leaving nothing for the next round until collisions generate more. Alternatively you could buy an upgrade instead of the Beacon, with the same immediate cost but a different long-term benefit.

Final round and limitations

In these three scenarios, round 8's arrival wave is empty, but charges still on the board may breach. Any remaining charges after movement also deal deadline damage equal to their strength. A Beacon cell may be charged on round 8, but an upgrade cannot become active that late. All three Beacon cells plus one online upgraded system and positive Hull are required for victory.

This redesigned solo prototype is intended to test economy, action consequences, and the win condition. It does not establish multiplayer cooperation, broad replayability, optimal balance, or fun. Human playtesting remains required before Phase 1 can be approved.
