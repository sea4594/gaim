How to play
Storm Engine

This is the complete rulebook for the browser-playable solo experiment. It is not the production Charge Loom v0.1 ruleset or a finished 1–4-player game.

Start a game →

Object and setup

Restore any two of Dynamo, Aegis and Switchyard to level 2 by the end of round 8 and keep Hull above zero. You start with Hull 6, Power 2, Coolant 2. All systems start at level 0. Your storm pattern contains eight rounds of charges, visible as each round begins. All resources and all decisions are public.

The board

Three lanes: Left, Center, Right. Each lane has two displayed positions: FAR (depth 2) and NEAR (depth 1). Incoming charges begin at FAR. After interventions, each charge advances one position toward the breach unless held this round. A charge moving from NEAR breaches and disappears. Charges at FAR do not breach that round unless they are already at NEAR when movement begins.

Each charge has a positive (+) or negative (−) polarity and strength 1–3. Multiple charges may occupy the same lane and time band. Only charges that breach in the same lane during the same round can cancel.

Round sequence (repeat eight times)

  1. Activate pending upgrades. Any upgrade purchased last round increases its system level by one now.
  2. Reveal the wave. Incoming charges enter at FAR. Previously present charges retain their positions and flags.
  3. Plan up to two paid charge edits. You may use none, one, or two. Each charge can receive only one paid edit this round. Edits execute in the order chosen.
  4. Optional construction. Spend exactly 2 Power to upgrade one system by one level (maximum level 2). You must have the Power before this round's collisions. No construction in a system that is offline this round. The upgrade takes effect at the start of the next round.
  5. Optional Switchyard move. If online, you may make one free Shift in addition to two paid edits (see below).
  6. Resolve. All non-held charges move one band. All charges reaching breach resolve simultaneously by lane. Apply collision Power, defense, damage and outages.
  7. Check defeat and deadline. Hull 0 or less ends the game immediately. After round 8, remaining charges also inflict damage equal to their combined strength; then test the victory objective.

Charge edits

SHIFT moves one charge to an adjacent lane, left or right. It cannot leave the board. Shift does not change time position or strength.

HOLD stops one charge from advancing this round, and increases its strength by one (maximum 3). A charge can be held only once per game. A previously held charge cannot be held again. Holding a dangerous charge trades immediate safety for a larger future problem.

DAMP costs 1 Coolant and reduces a charge's strength by one. A charge reduced to 0 is removed from the board. You cannot spend Coolant generated in the current round (the experimental Engine has no passive coolant generation).

Collisions and leakage

For each lane, total the strengths of all positive breaches and all negative breaches separately. The matched strength is the smaller total; it generates that much Power. The difference is leakage in that lane. Add Power from all three lanes, capped at 8 stored Power, after resolving damage.

Example: Left breaches with +2 and −1. One strength cancels, generating 1 Power. The remaining +1 is 1 leakage. Center and Right resolve independently.

Add all lane leakage to find raw damage. Aegis reduces that round's total raw damage (not leakage in each lane separately). Hull decreases by the remaining damage. A successful collision does not automatically repair Hull.

Three systems

  • Dynamo: Level 1 produces +1 bonus Power if a collision matches in Left. Level 2 produces +1 bonus Power if any lane matches. Bonus is at most +1 per round.
  • Aegis: Absorbs up to 1 raw damage per level, globally each round. Level 2 reduces up to 2 total raw damage.
  • Switchyard: Allows one extra free Shift per round. At level 1, it may target only FAR charges; at level 2, it may target any charge. The free Shift occurs after paid interventions and cannot move a removed charge or shift outside the board.

System outages

If 2 or more strength leaks in a lane in one round, the corresponding system (Left = Dynamo, Center = Aegis, Right = Switchyard) is offline during the following round. An offline system provides no effect and cannot receive construction that round. Outages last one round unless a new leak of 2 or more occurs in that same lane. The system's purchased levels are retained. Pending construction activates at the start of a round even if the system suffered an outage, but the offline system cannot use its power that round.

Final round

The wave in round 8 is empty in these test scenarios. Once normal movement and leakage resolve, any charges still left anywhere on the board deal damage equal to their combined strength: this is the deadline liability. If Hull survives, win only if at least two level-2 systems were online during round 8. A new upgrade ordered in round 8 will not activate before the game ends. You lose if Hull runs out at any point or the objective is not met on time.

Limits of the current prototype

This solo game is run entirely on a fixed set of transparent storms and accepts manual decisions; it has no bot opponent or random hidden reveal. It does not implement the experimental four-worker staffing system, cards, multiplayer, simultaneous commitments, or the production game. An action that technically matters may still fail to be fun. The point is to make those weaknesses experienceable and testable.
