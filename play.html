Your mission, in 60 seconds

Complete rules & examples ↗
  1. Survive eight rounds. Start with 6 Hull, 2 Power and 2 Coolant. Reach zero Hull and you lose.
  2. Match charges. + and − arriving together in one lane cancel. Each matched strength produces one Power; unmatched strength threatens Hull.
  3. Make up to two interventions. SHIFT a charge to an adjacent lane, HOLD it one round (it grows stronger), or DAMP it by one strength (costs Coolant).
  4. Build strategically. Use your project slot to spend 2 Power on a system upgrade OR one Beacon cell. Upgrades activate next round; the Beacon needs three cells.
  5. Shield deliberately. Spend up to 2 Power to absorb leakage damage. Aegis increases the protection per Power spent, but shields never work automatically.
  6. Win at the deadline. Finish round 8 alive with three Beacon cells charged and at least one upgraded system online. Remaining charges at the end still damage Hull.

Storms enter at the FAR row and move toward the NEAR row. NEAR charges will breach when you resolve this round unless held. The next incoming wave is previewed for planning. Upgrade Dynamo for energy, Aegis for efficient shielding, or Switchyard for flexible charge movement.

What should this prototype prove?

Whether resource commitments, competing Beacon and infrastructure investments, and a visible forecast produce an understandable, interesting eight-round game. This does not test human cooperation: there are no multiplayer controls here. We will not claim GAIM is production-ready until several people can learn, play and want to replay it.
