Your mission, in 60 seconds

Complete rules & examples ↗
  1. Survive eight rounds. Start with 6 Hull, 2 Power and 2 Coolant. Reach zero Hull and you lose.
  2. Match charges. + and − arriving together in one lane cancel. Each matched strength produces one Power; unmatched strength damages Hull.
  3. Make up to two interventions. SHIFT a charge to an adjacent lane, HOLD it one round (it grows stronger), or DAMP it by one strength (costs Coolant).
  4. Build strategically. Spend 2 Power to upgrade Dynamo, Aegis, or Switchyard. Upgrades start next round, and each system has two levels.
  5. Win at the deadline. Finish round 8 alive with at least two systems at level 2. Remaining charges at the end also damage Hull.

Storms enter at the FAR row and move toward the NEAR row. NEAR charges will breach when you resolve this round unless held. Your next incoming wave is already visible.

What should this prototype prove?

Whether the tactical storm board and persistent construction make an understandable, interesting eight-round game. This does not test human cooperation: there are no multiplayer controls here. We will not claim GAIM is production-ready until several people can learn, play and want to replay it.
