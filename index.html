02 / THE SYSTEM

Simple verbs. Hard consequences.

↔

Shift

Move a charge sideways to build or avoid a collision.

Ⅱ

Hold

Delay a charge, but increase its strength and future danger.

−

Damp

Spend coolant to weaken an incoming threat.

⇄

Cooperate

Up to four shared interventions. The best plans often require combined edits.
