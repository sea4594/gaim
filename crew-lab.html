What this proves—and what it doesn't

These are precomputed outcomes from the real Phase 6 experimental Python reducer, not a second gameplay engine in the browser. The finale also simulates the following empty round to check the actual win condition. No server or account is involved.

All seats can make distinct commitments, but many choices are illegal, ineffective, or obvious. This lab does not prove four-player fun, fair agency, or quarterbacking resistance. Test with four people, note who suggests each plan, and record when someone feels unnecessary.
